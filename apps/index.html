DSAI 601: Regression — Interactive Demos

Small interactive apps demonstrating specific topics from the course. Each link opens a standalone page.

  • Ordinary Least Squares Fit

    Click to add points and watch the OLS line and residual sum of squares update live.

  • The SSE Surface

    See the sum of squared residuals as a genuine function of β₀ and β₁ — a rotatable 3D bowl and a matching contour map, with sliders marking exactly where your chosen coefficients sit on it.

  • Sampling Distributions of β₀ and β₁

    1000 simulated samples confirm the theoretical sampling distributions of β̂₀ and β̂₁, and a forest plot of confidence intervals shows coverage matching the nominal confidence level.

  • Regression Diagnostics

    Residuals vs. fitted, Normal Q-Q, a histogram of standardized residuals, and residuals vs. leverage — all on the same simulated dataset, with draggable points and severity sliders for heteroscedasticity, nonlinearity, and skew.

  • Multiple Regression: 3D Plane Fit

    Drag to rotate a 3D scatter of two predictors and an outcome, with the OLS-fitted regression plane rendered as a wireframe mesh.

  • Dummy Variables & Interactions

    Two groups, a no-interaction model (forced parallel lines) and an interaction model (separate slopes) fit side by side — turn up the true slope difference and watch the parallel-lines model visibly break.

  • Multicollinearity & VIF

    Slide the correlation between two predictors and watch 150 resampled coefficient estimates stretch into an unstable ridge.

  • Logistic Regression

    Draggable two-class points with a live decision boundary and probability contours, linked to a sigmoid curve panel showing each point's fitted probability.

  • MLR Fundamentals Quiz

    Twenty multiple-choice/true-false questions reviewing matrix notation, the hat matrix, the F-test family, adjusted R², and feature engineering — click an answer to see if you're right, and why.
